Land rates

Every owner of land in Mto Safi pays land rates once a year. The rates pay for roads, street lights, markets and waste collection.

How your land rates are calculated

The rate is a share of the rateable value of your plot. The valuation roll sets that value, and your valuation notice states it. The share depends on what the land is used for.

Pay before 31 March. From April, interest is added for every month the rate stays unpaid.

How to pay

Pay by mobile money, by card in the e-services portal, or at the cash office in the municipal office. Keep the receipt: it is your proof of payment.
